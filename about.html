How it works

Three steps to a clear plan

Usually about two weeks from invitation to report.

  1. 1

    ROI Insights

    We model what the investment is worth to you: ROI, payback and value by area, from a few facts about your licences and tools.

    About 2 working days

  2. 2

    Questionnaire

    The people who know answer a short questionnaire. Answers save as you go, and “not sure” is always a valid answer.

    About 25–50 minutes each

  3. 3

    Evaluation report

    Your consultant combines both into a report with your score, the gaps behind it and a 90-day plan, then walks you through it.

    Within a week of your last answers

  4. →

    Next steps

    Your consultant agrees the next steps with you, such as trials, proofs of concept or hands-on workshops.

    Agreed with you

Better together
